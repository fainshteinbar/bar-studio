Which AI video brings you customers?

Most business AI videos open on a logo, and viewers scroll past before they know what they're watching. On my page, a video that opens on an ice cream tub from the freezer reached 224K views, 51 times the median Reel on this site. Films that open on a logo get, on average, a small fraction of that.

The difference isn't budget or tools. It's what the viewer sees in the first second.

What works

3 rules for the first second

  1. 01

    Open on something your customer knows, not your logo

    Viewers decide within a second whether to stay. A logo tells them nothing. Something they know from home, the supermarket line or their phone tells them: this is about me.

    In the example An ice cream tub that's in almost every Israeli freezer, right in the first frame. 224K views
  2. 02

    The twist lands in the first second

    Once they recognize it, surprise them, and fast. A surprise at second five arrives when they're already on the next video.

    In the example It opens on the ending: a guy walks out of a 7-Eleven with half a shelf. Only then do you learn he went in for a bottle of water. 45K views
  3. 03

    Your brand comes in after you've hooked them

    Your product isn't the hook. It's the answer. Once the viewer is curious, the brand closes the story instead of opening it.

    In the example First the face of someone hearing "139 shekels?!". Only then does the cake box open. 160K views
